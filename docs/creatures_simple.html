It creeps and spins at roughly a tenth of what the body can do. Every strategy-level theory was built on top of a policy that could not cross the pitch.

2. It is an offense problem

Against a half-strength bot the win rate stayed flat (17.1% → 17.4%) while 31 points of losses turned into draws, one for one. Scoring is pinned near 17% no matter who is on the other side — so it is not perception, and not opponent difficulty.

3. Small evaluations lie

Ten repeat evaluations of the same model returned anywhere from 11% to 24%. That is ordinary sampling noise, not progress. One lucky 24% was briefly committed as an improvement over 18% when the true mean was 16.2%. Evaluations now default to 1000 episodes.
