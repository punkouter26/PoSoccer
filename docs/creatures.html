2. Actuation — there are no joints

PoSoccer has no articulated rig: no joint chain, no DoF limits, no Kp/Kd drive gains. Each agent is a single Rigidbody2D driven by one linear force and one torque. This is a recorded deviation from the active-ragdoll brief — see rules-exemptions.md §2. What is documented below is the traction model, which is what actually turns actions into motion.

Measured chassis: 4.35 m/s jog, 9.54 m/s sprint, t95 ≈ 3.7 s. In-plane gravity is (0,0) (top-down — rules-exemptions.md §1), but g = 9.81 enters physically through the traction budget: body weight sets the normal force, which sets how hard the feet can push, cut and brake.

Ball: FIFA spec — r = 0.11 m, 0.43 kg, drag ~0.1 randomized per episode, Magnus curl.

Bottleneck analysis

  • Locomotion, not strategy. The chase probe measured the p8 policy at 2.62 m travelled in 4 s, 0.95 m/s top speed, never reaching the ball, against a chassis capable of 9.54 m/s and a bot that covers 15.08 m and arrives in 2.70 s. Every "cannot see / cannot finish / will not attack" theory was built on a policy that could not cross the pitch.
  • Offense, not perception or opponent strength. Halving the bot left the win rate flat (17.1% → 17.4%) and converted 31 points of losses into draws one-for-one. Scoring is pinned near 17% regardless of opponent.
  • Curriculum starvation. A gated ladder trapped weak policies on the easiest lesson — p7 spent all 20M steps at bot_strength 0.2 and was graded at 1.0. Coverage went backwards across runs (p5 0.8 → p6 0.5 → p7 0.2 → p8 0.35). Phase 9 replaces gating with uniform sampling over [0.2, 1.0].
  • Compute is a disproven lever. p4 (7.0M) and p5 (30.0M) both mean ~16–17%; 4.3× the steps bought nothing.
  • Entropy / action clipping: no collapse observed. beta 8.0e-3 with epsilon 0.2; actions are clamped to ±1 after a 1.6× ActionGain that lifts cautious brain outputs above the drag cutoff without changing the contract.
Statistical floor. Ten repeat evaluations of the identical model measured 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 15, 17, 18, 23, 24 — ordinary binomial noise (SD ≈ 3.7 at n = 100). Never rank two models on single 100-episode runs, and never read a <10-point gap as signal. evaluate.ps1 -Episodes defaults to 1000.
