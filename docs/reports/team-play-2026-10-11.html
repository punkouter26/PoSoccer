PoSoccer team-play training report

Generated 2026-10-11 · runs soccer_p31_standard, soccer_p32pass_standard, soccer_p33team_standard · graded over 350 matches each

After team training the brain completes 100 passes per 350 two-a-side matches, up from 11, and wins 29.7% of them, up from 21.7%. At one-a-side, the benchmark, it wins 37.1%, against 30.3% before. It still passes less than the scripted bots (182), and 10 of its 104 goals came from a teammate's pass.

A completed pass here means the ball went from one player to a teammate over at least 3 m with no opponent touching it in between.

2. Share of goals set up by a teammate (PoSoccer/blue_assisted_goal_rate)

Passing only matters if it leads to goals. This chart shows how often a goal came straight from a teammate's pass.

What the numbers on the picture mark

  1. Each point is the fraction of the trained side's goals, in that stretch of training, whose last two touches were two teammates.
  2. The line is jumpy because goals are rare: a single assisted goal moves a point a long way.
  3. Read the level, not the wiggles: both runs sit in a low band.
  4. The second run reads lower here, yet in the graded matches it scored twice as many assisted goals. The two runs trained against different bot strengths, so their curves are not like for like; the grades are.

How to read this, and what is still missing

  • What worked: starting from a brain that could already play, then training two-a-side against bots that hold positions and pass.
  • What the second run added: sharing the goal reward more evenly between scorer, passer and teammate. Graded passes and assisted goals both rose, and so did the one-a-side win rate. It was one run, so how much is the reward change and how much is simply more training is not separated.
  • Why mean reward is not shown: on this project it has repeatedly disagreed with the graded result, so it is not used to judge a run.
  • Not measured: whether passes are chosen at the right moment. The counts say passes happen more often, not that each one was the better option than shooting.
  • Where the files are: grades in results/eval/*.json, curves in results/<run>/STANDARD/, history in docs/training-history.md.
